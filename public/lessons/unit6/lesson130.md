# Lesson 130: To Have No Choice But To: ~ㄹ/을 수밖에 없다

## Vocabulary

### Nouns
- **상황** = situation, circumstance
- **결과** = result, outcome
- **선택** = choice, option
- **현실** = reality
- **포기** = giving up, abandonment
- **실패** = failure
- **수단** = means, method

### Verbs
- **포기하다** = to give up
- **인정하다** = to admit, to acknowledge
- **받아들이다** = to accept
- **선택하다** = to choose, to select

### Adjectives
- **불가피하다** = to be inevitable, unavoidable

## Introduction

In this lesson, you will learn how to use ~ㄹ/을 수밖에 없다 to express that you have "no choice but to" perform an action or that a certain outcome is inevitable given the circumstances.

## Expressing "No Choice But To": ~ㄹ/을 수밖에 없다

~ㄹ/을 수밖에 없다 is attached to verb or adjective stems. It is a combination of:
- ~ㄹ/을 수 있다/없다 (can / cannot)
- ~밖에 (nothing outside of / except)

When combined, it literally means "there is no option/way except to [verb]".

### Formation
- Stems ending in a vowel: **+ ㄹ 수밖에 없다** (가다 → 갈 수밖에 없다)
- Stems ending in a consonant: **+ 을 수밖에 없다** (먹다 → 먹을 수밖에 없다)

### Example Sentences

- 지금 상황에서는 그 계획을 **포기할 수밖에 없어요**.
  = In the current situation, we have no choice but to give up on that plan.

- 비밀이 밝혀졌으니 사실을 **인정할 수밖에 없어요**.
  = Since the secret was revealed, I have no choice but to admit the truth.

- 버스가 이미 떠나서 택시를 **탈 수밖에 없었어요**.
  = The bus had already left, so I had no choice but to take a taxi.

- 너무 열심히 공부했으니까 시험에 **합격할 수밖에 없어요**.
  = She studied so hard that she is bound to pass the exam.

- 아무도 돕지 않아서 혼자 **할 수밖에 없어요**.
  = Nobody is helping, so I have no choice but to do it by myself.
